Who Buys? Logistic Regression on E-commerce Sessions

Predicting whether a website visit ends in a purchase from 12,330 sessions (UCI dataset).

  • PageValues raises the odds of buying by 8.2% per unit; visits near holidays buy less (6.2% vs 16.5%).
  • Held-out test set: ROC AUC 0.863, close to in-sample results, so no overfitting.
  • Showed that 88% accuracy hides a 34% recall; a 0.2 threshold finds almost 2 in 3 buyers for a 1-point accuracy cost.

Was 2024 Unusually Hot? Nakhon Pathom Temperature

14,606 three-hourly readings from the Thai Meteorological Department, 2021–2025.

  • Parsed raw web-page exports (Thai cp874 encoding) and validated gaps, duplicates and daily averages before analysis.
  • 2024 was warmer than every year of 2021–2023 in 7 of 12 months; 2025 in none.
  • Welch t-tests adjusted for day-to-day autocorrelation instead of trusting naive p-values.
